# Trade review: 2026-09-11

The dashboard showed **"New entries paused — circuit breaker tripped"** with two reasons: *daily realized
loss 22.2% >= 20% limit* and *3 consecutive losses*. This is what actually happened, why, and what changed.

All figures come from read-only queries against the production database at ~18:45 UTC on 2026-09-11.
Times are UTC. The wallet is LIVE, funded with $28.60 on 2026-09-09; equity was $27.64 after the last close.

## Summary

- **15 live trades, net −$1.46** (7 wins +$10.22, 8 losses −$11.68). Gas cost another $1.46 on top.
- **The 22.2% was overstated.** The real realized loss was 5.3% of equity. Four winning partial sells made
  before the ledger fix (bde8ed7) never wrote a `REALIZED_PNL` row, so the breaker saw −$6.10. The
  3-loss streak was real.
- **The losses came from entries, not exits.** Two patterns account for $11.66 of the $11.68 lost: chasing a
  token that had just gone vertical (4 trades, −$9.90) and buying brand-new tokens while they were being
  dumped (3 trades, −$1.76).
- **Exits mostly weren't too early.** 6 of the 8 losers were at 0.01x–0.21x of our entry within hours. The
  opposite mistake happened twice: two winners (TUMBLE, PERPSHOOD) were sold well before their peak.
- **Broken sells made it worse.** Sells were failing until the float-precision fix went live at ~13:22.
  BLACKHOLE's stop hit at −25% at 13:04; it sold at −65% at 13:22.
- **Some of our own trade data is wrong.** Five fills landed 39–84% below DexScreener's displayed price, which
  inflated unrealized PnL and MFE on those trades, and the AI postmortems built on them.

partial sells didn't. Four winning partials happened before that fix:

Ledger total −$6.10 + $4.64 = −$1.46, which matches the trades table exactly: 5.3% of equity, well under the

entries would have paused anyway.

A one-time backfill of those four rows would correct today's figure. It has **not** been done — it's a
production database write.

## Where the money went

### Chasing a vertical move — 4 trades, −$9.90

BLACKHOLE, PONSIBLE, THREE, MARRONA.

- By our own snapshots each had already run **+37% to +54% in the 10 minutes before we bought**. Every winner
  was bought after a 0–8% run-up.
- Each was re-planned from WAIT_FOR_ENTRY up to BUY_NOW as the price climbed: BLACKHOLE three plans in 2
  minutes ($45.9K → $69.5K), THREE three in 9 minutes, PONSIBLE two in 2 minutes, MARRONA two in the same
  minute.
- Buyers were no more than sellers (5m buy ratio 48–49% on BLACKHOLE and PONSIBLE, 1h 42% on MARRONA) — that's
  distribution, not a breakout. Three of the four had RSI at ~100. The planning prompt tells the AI that RSI
  near 100 *with sustained buy pressure* is a breakout worth buying; it acted on the first half without the
  second.
- DexScreener's 5m change lags on this chain: it showed BLACKHOLE at −9% while our snapshots showed +46%.

### Buying brand-new tokens while they were being dumped — 3 trades, −$1.76

PONSFLY, Sheared, FFSTR.

- 2–8 minutes old, all let in by the momentum override, AI risk score 75–85.
- All three were WAIT_FOR_ENTRY pullback plans whose zone fired because price fell *into* it — the "pullback"
  was the dump. Sheared's own plan said to wait for stabilisation.
- Each went to 0.01x–0.14x of our entry shortly after.

These stayed small only because the liquidity-drop and stop exits fired fast. Four of the day's "winners"
(ladybug, OPAI, moltfly, INVEST) were the same kind of token and collapsed straight after we sold — luck, not
edge.

### Sells that couldn't go through

For much of the day sells were failing on-chain (the float-precision bug fixed in e0de2cd). The fix went live
around 13:22: four stuck positions sold within 51 seconds (13:22:46–13:23:37).

- **BLACKHOLE:** the −15% stop first registered at 13:04 (−24.7%); the sell went through at 13:22 at −65.4% —

- **QUORUM:** the catastrophic stop registered at 11:22 (−33%); it sold at 13:23 (−35%) after dipping to −64%

- **STONKBROKER:** its 60-minute time exit was due at 00:46; it sold at 13:23.
- **ladybug:** the AI position review called EXIT_NOW seven times from 04:34; none of them executed.

Stops that did execute still slipped: THREE sold at −20.7%, PONSIBLE at −25.6%, FFSTR at −17.6% against a
−15% stop. MARRONA went from −7% to −57% between two snapshots — no stop catches a one-block liquidity pull.

## What changed: conservative mode

When a **loss** breaker trips (daily realized loss or the losing streak), new entries no longer stop. The bot
keeps detecting, planning and sniping, and only buys when the setup clears every check below at the moment of
entry. Exits are unchanged. The kill switch, low gas and max open positions still pause entries completely.

| Check | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Token age | at least 60 min | Candidates under an hour old fell 50%+ within 24h in 74–85% of cases; over a day old, 16%. All nine of today's trades that went to near zero were under 30 minutes old. |
| Liquidity | at least $40K | Seven of those nine had less. Candidates with $50K+ fell 50%+ in 35% of cases, against 54% overall. |
| Activity | at least 30 trades in the last hour | Enough real two-way flow to price the token. |
| 1h buy ratio | 55–80% buys | Mattered most of everything tested. ~50% is distribution (BLACKHOLE, FFSTR, OPAI); over 80% is usually bots ahead of a dump. |
| 1h volume vs liquidity | at most 5x | Wash and churn: PONSFLY traded 27x its liquidity in an hour, OPAI 40x. |
| Price change | 1h between −30% and +500%; 5m between −15% and +30% | Not already vertical, not already collapsing. |
| Run-up in our own snapshots | at most +30% over 10 min | The chasing losers had run +37% to +54%; the winners 0–8%. |
| Drop from the 10-min high | at most 20% | Falling knives: FFSTR −31%, Sheared −25%. |
| AI plan | not PARABOLIC, DISTRIBUTION, PULLBACK, TRENDING_DOWN or UNKNOWN; risk score under 85 | Every trade today with those labels collapsed; plans scored 85+ fell 50%+ 68% of the time. |
| On-chain quote | no more than 10% below DexScreener's price | The stale-price check (table above). |

How it behaves:

- A setup that fails is **held, not rejected**. It's re-checked every few seconds while its plan is live,
  because a rejected candidate is never planned again. Whenever the failing checks change, a
  `conservative_gate` decision is saved on the candidate, so the dashboard shows why it's still waiting.
- Buys made in conservative mode are tagged *"conservative mode: passed the high-conviction gate"* in their BUY
  decision, so we can measure later whether they pay.
- AI re-entries (buying more of an open position on a dip) are off.
- **Hard stop:** past a 35% daily loss or 5 losses in a row, entries pause completely again.
- **How it ends:** the daily loss resets at midnight UTC; the losing streak clears when a trade closes in profit.
  Before, a losing streak paused entries until someone stepped in; now a high-conviction winner can clear it.
- The dashboard banner turns amber: *"Conservative mode — a loss limit tripped, so only high-conviction entries go through."*
- Every threshold is an env var (`CONSERVATIVE_*`, documented in `src/trading/config.ts`).
  `CONSERVATIVE_MODE_ENABLED=false` restores the old full pause.

### What it would have done

- **On 2026-09-11:** blocked all 15 trades. That avoids the $11.68 of losses and also skips the $10.22 of wins —
  mostly the lucky ones, but TUMBLE too.
- **Across 340 candidates** (24h outcomes, measured from each token's market cap when we first found it):

| | Passed | 1.5x within 24h | 2x within 24h | Fell 50%+ within 24h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All candidates | 340 | 39% | 27% | 54% |
| Market checks only | 20 | 45% | 20% | 5% |
| Candidates with an AI plan | 210 | 34% | 21% | 45% |
| Full gate, on those | 12 | 33% | 8% | 0% |

It gives up most moonshots in exchange for not getting rugged: on this chain the 2x+ moves mostly come from
brand-new tokens that rug just as often. Samples are small (12–20), so re-check the thresholds as data grows.
About 7–8 candidates a day passed the market checks when they were researched.

## How this was measured

- Read-only SQL against Trade, TradeExecution, PositionSnapshot, MarketSnapshot, TradeDecisionSnapshot,
  TradePostmortem, LedgerEntry, TradeCandidate, CandidateOutcome and StrategyVersion.
- "After our exit" uses MarketSnapshot rows from candidate outcome polling, up to ~17:45.
- Token ages use `max(minutes since firstSeenAt, minutes since pairCreatedAt − 60)`.
- Candidate outcomes measure the move from the market cap when a token was first found, not from an entry, over
  24h windows across ~2.6 days of data.
